Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

CASAVELLE (“we”, “us” or “our”) respects your privacy. This starter policy explains how information may be used when you browse our website, create an account or enquire about a mirror. Review and adapt this policy to your actual business practices before public launch.

Information we collect

If you create an account or place an order, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, order details and messages you choose to send us. Passwords are stored as salted password hashes by the website backend, not as plain text.

How we use information

  • To manage accounts and respond to product enquiries.
  • To confirm orders, delivery details and after-sales support.
  • To maintain security, prevent misuse and improve the store.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with delivery or technology providers only where necessary to provide a requested service, or where required by law. WhatsApp is a separate service and its own terms and privacy practices apply when you contact us there.

Retention and security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no website can guarantee absolute security. Keep hosting, administrator credentials and backups secure. Do not upload sensitive personal information through this site.

Your choices

You may contact us to request correction or deletion of account information, subject to records we are legally required to retain.
